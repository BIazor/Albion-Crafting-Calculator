Honest return rates

Focus cost per craft

Planner sessions

Three steps from recipe to profit

The whole workflow lives on one screen, and the numbers update as you type.

  1. Choose what you're crafting Set the tier and enchantment, then pick an item from the sidebar. Icons update to the real in-game artwork.
  2. Plug in return rate and prices Set your hideout, focus and daily bonus, then fill in material and sell prices for the tier you're working.
  3. Send it to the planner Queue anything profitable into a session and the planner totals the materials, journals, focus and profit in one go.
