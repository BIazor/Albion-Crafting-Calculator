What the calculator settles.

Every figure comes from the recipe you picked, the return rate you set and the prices you typed. Nothing is guessed, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Materials after your return
Hideout power level, zone quality, focus and the daily bonus all change how much of each ingredient comes back — and what you actually have to buy.
Focus, per craft and per session
Your Destiny Board specialisation is part of the efficiency formula, so focus is counted for a single craft and for the whole planner.
Tax, margin and the best craft
Enter the buy and sell prices you see in game. The sales tax you choose is applied to revenue, and every tier and enchantment is ranked by margin.
A planner that keeps up
Queue the crafts worth running, set quantities and journals, and materials, artefacts and focus stay in one running total for the session.
Your data stays put
No account and no server. Prices, stock and planner sessions are saved in this browser, so the page comes back the way you left it.

Three steps from recipe to margin.

The whole workflow lives on one screen. You bring the prices you see in game; the numbers update as you type.

  1. Choose the item

    Set the category, tier and enchantment. The recipe, its material list and the item art follow the selection.

  2. Set your return

    Give it your hideout's power level and zone quality, whether you're using focus, and whether the daily bonus is up.

  3. Read the margin

    Type what the materials cost and what the item sells for. Investment, revenue and margin update as you type.
