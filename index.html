What it accounts for

Every figure on the calculator comes from the recipe you selected, the return rate you set and the prices you entered — nothing is guessed.

Recipe and return rate
All the ingredients for the tier and enchantment you pick, with hideout power level, zone quality and the daily bonus folded into the materials you really have to buy.
Focus per craft
Your Destiny Board specialisation feeds the efficiency formula, so focus is shown per item, per craft and across a whole session.
Prices you enter
Type the buy and sell prices you see in game. The calculator applies the sales tax you choose and returns your investment, sell value, margin and a ranked best-to-craft list.
Planner sessions
Queue the crafts worth running, set quantities and journals, and the planner keeps materials, transport and focus in one running total.
Local by default
Everything is stored in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking: close the tab and your sessions are still there when you come back.

From recipe to profit

The whole workflow fits on one screen: the numbers change as you type, so you can see what a price does to the margin before you commit.

  1. Pick the item Set the tier and enchantment, then choose from the catalogue. The recipe and its material list follow your selection.
  2. Enter your prices and return rate Metals, leather, planks and cloth get the prices you pay; the sell price goes on the other side. Hideout settings and focus decide how much of it you get back.
  3. Keep what is worth crafting Send profitable crafts into a session and the planner totals the materials, journals and focus for the whole night.
